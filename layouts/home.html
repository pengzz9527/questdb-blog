❓ QuestDB 常见问题

QuestDB 适合什么场景？

QuestDB 最适合时间序列场景：IoT 传感器数据、金融 tick/K线数据、可观测性指标与日志。它擅长高吞吐写入与亚毫秒级聚合查询，不适合一般事务处理（OLTP）或频繁更新的低延迟点查，那是 PostgreSQL 的领域。

QuestDB 和 InfluxDB 哪个更好？

两者都面向时序，但侧重点不同。QuestDB 在高并发摄入 + 低延迟分析查询上通常更强，且原生支持标准 SQL 与 Grafana；InfluxDB 在云生态与托管服务上更成熟。选型应取决于你的写入速率、查询延迟要求与运维成本。

QuestDB 能替代 ClickHouse 吗？

部分场景可以。QuestDB 专为时序设计，写入吞吐和时间聚合通常更快且更易用；ClickHouse 在通用 OLAP 与复杂多维分析上更灵活。如果你的数据以时间序列为主，QuestDB 往往是更省心的选择。

QuestDB 能处理多大数据量？

单机即可支持每秒数百万行的高吞吐写入，配合 PARTITION BY DAY/MONTH 分区与压缩，可高效管理 TB 级历史数据。需要更大规模时，可使用 QuestDB 的分布式模式横向扩展。

❓ QuestDB FAQ

What is QuestDB best for?

Time-series workloads — IoT sensor data, financial tick/OHLC data, observability metrics, and logs. It excels at high-throughput ingestion and sub-millisecond analytical queries, but is not designed for general OLTP (high-concurrency transactions) or frequent low-latency point updates — that's PostgreSQL territory.

QuestDB vs InfluxDB: which is better?

Both target time-series, but with different emphases. QuestDB is typically stronger in high-concurrency ingestion + low-latency analytical queries, with native standard SQL and Grafana support; InfluxDB has a more mature cloud ecosystem and managed services. Choose based on your write rate, query latency requirements, and ops cost.

Does QuestDB replace ClickHouse?

In some scenarios, yes. QuestDB is purpose-built for time-series, with faster write throughput and time aggregation that's easier to use; ClickHouse is more flexible for general OLAP and complex multi-dimensional analytics. If your data is primarily time-series, QuestDB is often the more convenient choice.

How much data can QuestDB handle?

A single node supports millions of rows per second of high-throughput writes. With PARTITION BY DAY/MONTH partitioning and compression, it efficiently manages TB-scale historical data. For larger scale, use QuestDB's distributed mode to scale horizontally.
