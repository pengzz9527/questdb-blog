# Correction Policy

We welcome corrections. If you find an error in our content, please report it via:

- **Email**: admin@questdblab.org
- **GitHub Issues**: [questdblab](https://github.com/questdblab)

## Process

1. **Verify**: Within 48 hours of receiving a report
2. **Update**: Confirmed errors are fixed in the original article within 24 hours
3. **Log**: A "Corrections Log" section is appended to the article, noting the correction date and reason
4. **Notify**: Subscribers are notified via Telegram

## Corrections Log Format

Articles with corrections will have a section like this at the end:

> **Corrections Log**
> - 2026-11-01: Fixed ILP timestamp unit (was "milliseconds," actually nanoseconds)

## Principles

- No hidden edits — all substantive corrections are publicly logged
- Minor typos and punctuation fixes are silent (not a substantive correction)
- Performance data corrections require re-testing with new numbers attached
